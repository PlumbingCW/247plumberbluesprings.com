Faucet Repair in Blue Springs, MO

A drip is small until the water bill and the stained sink say otherwise. We fix the fault, not just the symptom.

Call (855) 575-2890

Faucet repair in Blue Springs, MO covers dripping spouts, leaks at the handle or base, low flow, loose handles and worn cartridges on kitchen, bath and utility faucets. Most repairs are a part swap and finish in a single visit.

If water is spraying from under the sink and not dripping from the spout, shut the angle stops under the sink first. A failed supply line is a leak and is handled like leak detection and burst pipe repair.

We replace the faucet only when repair does not make sense, and we say so plainly.

Faucet symptoms and what causes them

The fault usually shows in where the water appears.

Cleaning the aerator is a safe first step for low flow. Unscrew it, soak it in vinegar and rinse.

Hard water and faucet wear

Scale builds up inside cartridges and aerators where water evaporates and leaves minerals behind. The state-filed 2025 report for the City of Blue Springs lists carbonate hardness of 123 mg/L for the Independence component of the supply, about 7 grains per gallon by our conversion. The city blends three sources, so your tap may differ.

Moderately hard water is a common reason for faucets that stiffen, sputter or drip. A yearly aerator cleaning helps, and a water softener reduces buildup throughout the house.

What a drip actually costs

A faucet that drips once per second can add up to thousands of gallons per year, and the City of Blue Springs bills water by usage tiers per 100 gallons. A repair that costs less than a year of waste pays for itself.

Constant dripping also stains porcelain and wears the seat the cartridge seals against, which makes later repair harder.

Repair or replace the faucet

Consider replacement when any of these apply.

  • The faucet is more than 15 years old and parts are hard to find.
  • The finish is pitted, corroded or flaking.
  • The body itself leaks, not the seals.
  • You are remodeling and want a different style.
  • Repeated repairs in a short span.

If the faucet is in good condition, we repair it. Do not replace what a cartridge swap can fix.

Kitchen, bath and utility faucets

Kitchen faucets with a pull-down sprayer have a hose and a diverter that can leak under the sink. Bath faucets with separate hot and cold handles use compression stems that wear. A utility sink faucet often has a threaded spout and a washer. We stock the common parts and match by the make you tell us.

If a part is no longer made, we explain the replacement options and what they would cost before we proceed.

Faucet efficiency labels

The EPA WaterSense label on bathroom faucets marks models limited to 1.5 gallons per minute. If you replace a faucet, the label is a simple way to choose an efficient one that still performs well.
