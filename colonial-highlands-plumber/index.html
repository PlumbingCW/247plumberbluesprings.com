Colonial Highlands is a residential area in Blue Springs, MO 64014. Listings place homes on streets such as SE Colonial Drive, SE Linden Drive and SE Holly Drive, and a rental community called Colonial Highlands Villas is also listed. The name appears as the subdivision on home listings.

Homes in the area were not all built at the same time. At least one recent listing was built in 2020, so houses of different ages can sit on neighboring streets. That makes it worth asking how old each system in your own house is.

What is documented about Colonial Highlands

We confirmed the subdivision name on home listings, the ZIP code 64014, the street names above and the Colonial Highlands Villas rental community. One listing described a 2020 home with a finished walkout lower level and an unfinished sub-basement that could become a bedroom or workout room.

We do not have a published boundary map and we do not claim one. We found no reliable source for the age mix across the whole area, so we do not guess.

Mixed-age homes and mixed-age systems

In an area where new and older houses share streets, a plumber cannot assume anything from the address. A newer house can have a failed fixture and an older house can have a recent heater. The useful questions are when the water heater was installed, when the last drain problem happened and where the shutoffs are.

If you are replacing a heater, water heater installation explains what happens on the call. If it is leaking or not heating, see water heater repair.

Lower levels, walkouts and sump pumps

Walkout lower levels and unfinished sub-basements are common in this part of the Kansas City area. They add living space and also add plumbing, because a basement bathroom or laundry needs drains, a vent and sometimes a pump. If your lower level stays damp or has taken on water, sump pump service covers pits, check valves and battery backups. For adding a bathroom or fixture, see plumbing installation.

Questions to ask about your own house

These help you know what you have, whatever the age of the home.

  • When was the water heater installed?
  • Is there a sump pit and does the pump start when tested?
  • Where are the main shutoff and the sewer cleanout?
  • Are there capped lines in an unfinished level?

Write the answers on a note near the water heater.
