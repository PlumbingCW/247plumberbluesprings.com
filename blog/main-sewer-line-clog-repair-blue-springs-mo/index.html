Main Sewer Line Clog Repair: Blue Springs, MO Homeowner Guide

A main line clog shuts down the whole house. Here is how the diagnosis goes and what separates a one-time clog from a repair.

Call (855) 575-2890

By the Blue Springs 24/7 Plumbing team · Published and updated November 14, 2026

A main sewer line clog is a blockage in the pipe that carries all household wastewater to the city sewer. Because it sits downstream of every fixture, the symptoms are household-wide. Several drains slow at once, toilets gurgle, and the lowest drain in the house backs up first.

This guide explains how to tell a main clog from a local one, what a plumber does, and when a clog is really a symptom of a damaged pipe that needs repair.

If sewage is backing up now, stop using water and call our 24/7 line. The rest can wait.

Main line backup: what to do right now

These steps reduce the damage.

Stop using water.

Every fixture sends water into the blocked line.

Find the cleanout.

It is a capped pipe near the foundation or in the basement. Do not open it if sewage will pour out.

Protect the floor.

Lift belongings and rugs.

Call the 24/7 line.

We schedule the earliest visit available.

Why main lines clog

The usual causes are roots entering joints, grease that has cooled and coated the wall, flushed items that do not break down and bellies where the pipe has sagged. Older clay and cast iron lines are more prone to roots and scale. Newer plastic lines clog less often but can still collect grease and wipes.

If a line clogs again within months, the clog is a symptom. A camera finds the underlying issue, whether it is a belly, a crack or a root-filled joint.

The history helps.

A line that clogs on a schedule is telling you something. We listen.

The repair path

We clear the clog with a cable or jet, then run a camera to view the line. If the camera shows a sound pipe, we recommend a maintenance plan. If it shows damage, we explain repair options such as spot repair, lining or replacement and give a clear price. See sewer line repair and sewer inspection.

We give you the recording so you can see the problem for yourself. A repair decision should be informed, not rushed.

Preventing the next clog

Keep grease out of the kitchen drain, do not flush wipes, even those labeled flushable, and avoid planting trees directly over the sewer lateral. If your lateral has a root history, consider a periodic cleaning schedule and a camera check.

How a camera changes the decision

Without a camera, a clog repair is a guess. A camera shows whether the pipe is round, whether a joint has slipped, whether roots are present and where the line bellies. It also shows whether the cable actually cleared the problem or only punched a hole in it.

We record the inspection and walk you through it. You see what we see, and the repair decision rests on the picture.

Wipes, grease and what the line cannot take

Wipes marketed as flushable do not break down like toilet paper. They tangle around roots and rough joints and build a mat that catches everything else. Grease poured down a kitchen sink cools in the pipe and clings to the wall. Over months it narrows the line until a small piece of debris stops it.

The cheapest fix is a habit change. Wipes go in the trash and grease goes in a can.

When the city main is the problem

If every neighbor on your street has backups at the same time, the blockage is probably in the city sewer main. Call the City of Blue Springs water and sewer line at (816) 622-4444 and report it. Do not run a cable through the main, since you cannot clear the city line from your cleanout.

If only your house is affected, the blockage is on your lateral. That is the owner's repair, and we handle it.

We handle this kind of work across the area, including Lee's Summit and Grain Valley. Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see.

Who is responsible in Blue Springs

The City of Blue Springs water and sewer line is (816) 622-4444. In general the owner maintains the lateral from the house to the connection, and the city maintains its main. If many neighbors have backups, call the city.

Section 500.130 of the code ties plumbing permits to proof of license. A plumber doing excavation or a repair on your lateral should show a valid license.

Questions about this topic in Blue Springs

Do I call the city or a plumber for a main clog?

Start with a plumber for your lateral. If neighbors are affected too, also call the city at (816) 622-4444.

How do I find my cleanout?

Look for a capped pipe near the foundation, in a basement or in the yard along the sewer path.

Will a clog clear itself?

Rarely. Waiting often turns a clog into a backup.

Is a main line clog covered by insurance?

Typically not the clog itself. Resulting damage may be covered under a backup endorsement.

Can roots be killed with chemicals?

Some products exist, but a camera and a mechanical cut give the clearest result. Ask a plumber.
