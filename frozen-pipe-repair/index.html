Emergency Frozen Pipe Repair in Blue Springs, MO

A frozen pipe is a burst pipe waiting to thaw. Shut the main first if you see a split or bulge.

Call (855) 575-2890

Frozen pipe repair in Blue Springs, MO means safely thawing ice in a supply line and repairing any split before the water releases. If a faucet runs slow or stops in cold weather, the pipe may be frozen. Open the faucet and call the 24/7 line.

Do not use an open flame. A hair dryer or warm towels, applied slowly, are safer. If you see a split, bulge or water, shut the main valve and call.

Once a pipe thaws, a hidden split can release water fast. That is why you should call before the temperature rises. Burst pipes are handled on burst pipe repair.

Where Kansas City area pipes freeze

Exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages, attics and hose bibs are the usual spots. Pipes on the north side of a house and any run in an uninsulated space face the most exposure.

During a hard freeze, keep cabinet doors open under sinks on exterior walls, let a faucet trickle and keep the thermostat steady.

Preventing a repeat

Insulate exposed pipes, seal drafts near plumbing, disconnect and drain outdoor hoses and shut off exterior spigot lines before winter. If one pipe freezes repeatedly, we can reroute or add insulation.

What to do after the pipe thaws

Once flow returns, walk the house and check the pipe, the walls and the floor below for drips. A split is often small and weeps, so look closely at joints and at the side of the pipe facing the cold. Check the ceiling below a bathroom or kitchen for new stains. Leave the faucet open a trickle until you are sure the pipe is sound.

If you find a leak, close the main and call. A small split under pressure sprays a fine mist that soaks insulation and drywall quickly.

Prevention before the next hard freeze

Foam pipe sleeves are inexpensive and effective on exposed runs. Seal gaps where pipes enter the house with caulk or expanding foam, and close foundation vents in unheated crawlspaces during cold snaps. Disconnect garden hoses and shut off exterior hose bibs if they have interior shutoffs, then open the outside spigot to drain.

When a hard freeze is forecast, open the cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and let a faucet drip. Moving water is much harder to freeze, and a trickle costs far less than a repair.

Pipes that freeze again and again

If the same pipe freezes every winter, something about its location is wrong. It may run through an uninsulated wall, sit near an air leak or lack insulation in a crawlspace. We can add insulation, reroute the line toward the warm side of the house or install heat cable made for plumbing, where it makes sense.

A pattern is worth solving once. See burst pipe repair for what happens after a split.

Questions we hear from Blue Springs homeowners

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night in Blue Springs?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7. Open the faucet and call so we can schedule the earliest visit available.

Can I thaw a pipe myself?

Gently, with a hair dryer or warm towels. Never use a flame.

Will a frozen pipe always burst?

No, but a split can hide until it thaws. Call before it warms.

How do I stop pipes freezing again?

Insulate, seal drafts and keep a trickle running during hard freezes.

Is a frozen pipe covered by insurance?

A burst often is. A pipe that froze but did not break usually is not.
