We confirm the tank has failed, explain your options and quote a clear price before starting. If a part can fix it cheaply, we say so and point you to water heater repair.

Planned upgrades, such as moving from a tank to on-demand, are handled on water heater installation and tankless water heater.

Signs the tank is done

A failed tank leaks from the body, not from a fitting. Rust-colored water, a rumble that sounds like boiling and a unit older than a decade all point toward replacement. When the tank bottom has corroded through, no repair holds.

Check the data plate for the manufacture date. The serial number often encodes the month and year, and we can read it if you cannot.

What a replacement visit includes

We shut off water and fuel, drain and disconnect the old tank, set the new unit, connect water, gas or electric and the flue where applicable, fit a temperature and pressure relief valve and discharge pipe and test the system. We also cover disposal of the old tank.

Many Blue Springs homes built from the 1970s through the 1990s have water heaters in garages, basements or closets with older venting. We check that the venting, drain pan and clearances meet current requirements and explain any changes needed.

Permits and licensing

The Blue Springs code (Section 500.130) requires that plumbing permits be issued only when proof of license is available, and sets owner-occupant exemptions for work on a single-family home. Ask the Community Development Department whether a permit applies to your replacement.

In a hard water area, replacing the heater is also a good moment to talk about a water softener so the next tank lasts longer.

Safety checklist for a failed or leaking tank

Do these while you wait.

  • Shut off the cold supply and the gas or power.
  • Do not drain a hot tank, since the water can scald.
  • Move people and pets away from a leaking gas unit if you smell gas.
  • Do not relight a pilot in a room where you smell gas.
  • Keep towels and a bucket ready, and photograph damage for insurance.

Replacement follows the manufacturer instructions and local code. Gas units are installed to NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code, where it applies.

Questions we hear from Blue Springs homeowners

Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Blue Springs?

Yes. We answer 24/7. If a tank is leaking, shut off the water and fuel and call so we can schedule the earliest visit available.

How long does a water heater last?

Many tanks last roughly 8 to 12 years depending on water, maintenance and use. Moderately hard water shortens it without flushing.

Can I switch from gas to electric or to a heat pump model?

Often yes, but it depends on electrical capacity, venting and space. We assess your home before recommending.

Do you remove the old heater?

Yes. Removal and disposal are part of the replacement quote.

Will I need a permit?

Rules depend on the work. The city code ties permits to licensed contractors, so ask Community Development and confirm your plumber can show proof of license.
