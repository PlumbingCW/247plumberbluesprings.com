The calls Grain Valley sends us

A growing city draws a mix of housing, from older homes near the original town to newer subdivisions built as the area expanded along the interstate. Water heaters and winter in Grain Valley

Winter drops well below freezing in the Kansas City area, so exposed pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls are the usual places for a freeze split. A faucet that slows in a hard freeze is a warning, covered on frozen pipe repair.

Water heaters fail more often in cold months because they work harder. If the tank is leaking from the body, water heater replacement is the fix. If it only stopped heating, the repair is often a part.
