Stone Canyon is a golf course community in Blue Springs, MO 64015, on S Stone Canyon Drive. A new home listing describes it as about 300 acres around Stone Canyon Golf Club, an 18-hole course that opened in 2009 and was designed by Greg Norman. Listings show custom home builders marketing homes there.

This page is for homeowners in Stone Canyon. The homes here are newer than many in the older parts of Blue Springs, so the plumbing questions lean toward installations, water quality and larger layouts. This is general guidance and not a statement about any one home.

What is documented about Stone Canyon

The community is in ZIP code 64015 and its streets include S Stone Canyon Drive, S White Sands Court and 41st Terrace. We found no published build-year breakdown, so we do not state home ages. We can say that custom homes have been marketed there and that the golf course opened in 2009.

We do not have a published boundary map and we do not claim one.

Newer systems and what they need

Newer homes often have tankless or large-capacity water heaters, multiple bathrooms and more fixtures. Tankless units need periodic descaling in hard water. Blue Springs buys treated water from Independence, Kansas City and Tri County Water Authority, and the state-filed 2025 report lists carbonate hardness of 123 mg/L for the Independence supply, about 7 grains per gallon, which is moderately hard.

See tankless water heater service and water softener and filtration for how those pieces work. If you are planning a new heater, water heater installation explains the call.

Irrigation and backflow

Lawns around golf communities are often irrigated, and many irrigation systems have a backflow preventer that may need to be tested on a schedule set by the local water provider. We do not state the local rule here. Ask the City of Blue Springs what applies to your connection, then read about backflow testing and repair.

A seasonal checklist for a larger home

Larger houses have more places for a small problem to hide.

  • Shut off outdoor spigots and drain irrigation lines before the first hard freeze.
  • Look inside cabinets under sinks for damp spots every few months.
  • Flush a tankless heater on the schedule in its manual.
  • Know the location of the main shutoff and any zone shutoffs.

A small drip that is caught early costs less than a hidden leak.

What is verified and what is not

Verified from public sources: the community name, ZIP code 64015, the golf club opening in 2009, the Greg Norman design and the roughly 300 acre size reported in a builder listing.
