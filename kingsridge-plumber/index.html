Kingsridge is a residential area in Blue Springs, MO 64015. Records show a Kingsridge Drive and a multifamily property called Kingsridge West at 4209 NW Cardiff Drive, and real estate sites list Kingsridge as a neighborhood name.

The area mixes single-family houses with apartments, so the first question in a plumbing problem is often who is responsible for the repair. This page covers both homeowners and renters.

What is documented about Kingsridge

A neighborhood data profile reports that about 92 percent of residential real estate here was built between 1970 and 1999, with a smaller number from 1940 to 1969. It describes mainly single-family homes with some apartment buildings and a mix of owners and renters.

We do not have a published boundary map. We use the street names above as anchors and we do not claim an official boundary.

Homes built in the 1970s through the 1990s

Houses from this period are not new and not historic. Original water heaters have usually been replaced by now, and fixtures have often been updated one at a time. That is a pattern across many older suburbs, not a finding about Kingsridge specifically. The repairs that come up most often in homes like these are small ones, such as a dripping faucet, a running toilet or a slow drain.

See faucet repair, toilet repair and drain cleaning. If water is appearing where it should not, leak detection helps find the source before walls are opened.

Owners, landlords and renters

With apartments and houses side by side, the person who calls the plumber varies. Tenants should tell the landlord or property manager about a leak or backup in writing and keep a photo. Owners and landlords can call us directly. When several units share a line, a backup in one unit can be a symptom of a shared line, so tell us how many fixtures are affected.

A water heater that stops heating or leaks is covered on our water heater repair page.

What to tell us when you call

The more specific the description, the faster we can say what to do.

  • Your street and whether you own or rent.
  • Which fixtures are affected and when it started.
  • Whether water is spraying, dripping or only slow.
  • Whether other units or neighbors have the same problem.

If water is spraying, close the nearest shutoff first.

What is verified and what is not

Verified from public sources: the ZIP code 64015, Kingsridge Drive, the Kingsridge West multifamily property and the neighborhood name on real estate sites. Not verified: boundaries, how many houses or units exist and the condition of any pipe. For problems on the city side of your property, call (816) 622-4444.

Questions from Kingsridge homeowners

Is Kingsridge in Blue Springs?

I rent in Kingsridge. Can I call you?

You can call to ask what a symptom usually means, but repairs in a rented unit normally go through the landlord.

Describe the situation when you call. A shared line usually involves the property owner.
