Plumbing Service Area: Blue Springs, MO and Nearby Towns

We take calls for Blue Springs and eastern Jackson County. Find your town and the ZIP codes we cover.

Blue Springs 24/7 Plumbing is a call-first plumbing line for Blue Springs in Jackson County. Our home area is Blue Springs itself, ZIP codes 64014 and 64015. The line also takes calls from the nearby towns below.

Distances are estimates. Blue Springs to Grain Valley is a short drive east on Interstate 70, and Oak Grove is further along the same corridor. Lee's Summit and Independence are larger cities to the southwest and west. We tell you honestly how long dispatch to your address is expected to take.

Emergency service across the area

A burst pipe or a sewage backup does not wait for business hours, so the line is answered around the clock in every town on this page. Shut off the main water valve, stop using any drain that is backing up and call. We schedule the earliest visit available.

Each town page explains what its homes most often call about. Grain Valley leans toward drains and water heaters, Oak Grove toward freeze damage, Lee's Summit toward sewer laterals and Independence toward aging pipes.

Nearby areas we mention

Raytown, Pleasant Hill, Lone Jack and Grandview sit at the edges of what is realistic for dispatch. We do not have separate pages for them. If you live there, call and we confirm whether we can reach your address and how long it would take.

City utilities we point you to

For problems on the city side of your property, call the City of Blue Springs water and sewer line at (816) 622-4444. Some southern Blue Springs addresses are served by Jackson County PWSD 13, so check your bill. Everything inside your house and on your own sewer lateral is our work.

How to tell if your address is covered

If you live in Blue Springs or any of the four towns above, you are in our service area. If you live in a nearby place that is not listed, call and give us the street address and the nearest cross street. We look at the real drive and tell you plainly whether we can reach you and about how long it may take.

Interstate 70 is the spine of the eastern side of our area. Grain Valley and Oak Grove sit along it, so highway traffic and weather shape the drive. Lee's Summit and Independence are reached on surface roads and highways, and drive times change with the time of day.

What we ask when you call

We ask for the street address, the nearest cross street, what you see and what you have already done, such as closing the main water valve. If sewage is involved, we ask which fixtures are affected. That helps us send the right equipment the first time.

We also ask whether anyone is in danger. If you smell gas or see sparks near water, leave the area and call the gas utility or 911 first, then call us.

Neighborhoods inside Blue Springs

Inside Blue Springs we work in every part of the city, from the area around Burrus Old Mill Park and the original town site to the newer streets near Adams Dairy Parkway and the Missouri Route 7 corridor. Fleming Park, with Blue Springs Lake and Lake Jacomo, is a landmark we use when giving directions.

The home area also includes ZIP codes 64014 and 64015. Older homes in the city often have the original water heaters, supply lines and sewer laterals that cause most of our calls.

Seasonal patterns across the area

Winter brings frozen and split pipes, especially in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls. Spring brings sump pump failures and sewer backups when heavy rain loads the system. Summer brings irrigation leaks and water heater failures in hot garages, and fall is a good time for backflow tests and pre-winter pipe checks.
