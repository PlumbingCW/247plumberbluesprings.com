Why pipes burst in the Kansas City area

Kansas City area winters regularly drop well below freezing. When water in a pipe freezes it expands and splits the pipe, often at a weak point or a fitting, and the break stays closed until it thaws. That is why a burst pipe often announces itself with water hours after the temperature rises.

Exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages and outdoor hose bibs are the usual spots. Keeping a trickle running during a hard freeze and opening cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls helps. If a pipe is frozen but not yet burst, do not use an open flame to thaw it.

Frozen pipe warning signs before a burst

A frozen pipe gives hints before it breaks.

  • A faucet that suddenly runs slow or stops entirely in cold weather.
  • Frost on a visible pipe or a pipe cold to the touch.
  • A faint bulge or bowed section on exposed pipe.
  • A sound of rushing water that stops abruptly.
  • No water from a single fixture while others work.

If you see any of these, shut the main and call before the pipe thaws and releases water.

Copper, PEX and older pipe repair

We repair the pipe you have. Copper splits get a cut-out and a new section with proper fittings. Plastic supply lines such as PEX are repaired with approved couplings, and failing polybutylene lines found in some homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s are better replaced than patched.

Blue Springs grew from about 6,800 people in 1970 to about 40,200 by 1990, so some homes in that window may have older supply piping. If we find repeated failures, we explain whether repiping makes more sense than another patch.

Questions we hear from Blue Springs homeowners

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Blue Springs?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7 including holidays. Shut off the main water valve and call so we can schedule the earliest visit available.

Where is my main water shutoff?

It is where the service line enters the house, often in a basement, utility room, garage or crawlspace. Turn the handle clockwise until it stops.

Is it safe to turn the water back on after a repair?

Open the main slowly and check the repaired section and nearby joints for drips. We test the repair before leaving.

Will insurance cover a burst pipe?

Often the resulting water damage is covered when the break is sudden, and the pipe repair itself may not be. Check your policy.

How can I stop pipes from freezing next winter?

Insulate exposed pipes, disconnect outdoor hoses, seal drafts near plumbing and keep a trickle running during a hard freeze.
