Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation in Blue Springs, MO

A disposal that hums, jams or leaks usually has a simple cause. We find it and tell you whether to repair or replace.

Call (855) 575-2890

Garbage disposal repair in Blue Springs, MO covers jams, humming with no spin, leaks, reset-button trips, slow draining and noisy grinding, plus installation of a new unit. Turn the disposal off at the switch before you reach in, and never put a hand inside.

If the sink backs up with the disposal running, the clog may be in the line past the disposal. That is a drain issue, covered on drain cleaning.

We confirm the fault, quote a clear price and replace only what needs replacing.

Disposal symptoms and likely causes

The sound or sign usually points to the problem.

A leak from the housing body means the unit has failed and should be replaced.

What not to put down a disposal

Grease, coffee grounds in quantity, pasta, rice, potato peelings and fibrous vegetables such as celery and corn husks are the usual causes of slow drains. They bind, swell or coat the line, even when the disposal grinds them.

Run cold water during and after grinding. It keeps fats solid enough to flush through and prevents them from smearing on the pipe wall.

Installing a new disposal

A new disposal needs a sink flange, mounting assembly, drain connection and a power connection. We check that the dishwasher drain connects properly and that the unit is sized for the household.

Horsepower is a trade-off between noise, power and price. We recommend a size that suits how you cook and tell you what the extra cost buys.

Before you call

These checks help us diagnose faster.

  • Turn off the switch, then try the reset button under the unit.
  • Check whether the dishwasher also drains slowly.
  • Note any moisture under the sink.
  • Take the brand and model from the label if you can.
  • Tell us if it has jammed before.

If the unit is more than about ten years old and leaks, plan for replacement.

Safety first with any disposal

Never put your hand in a disposal, even when it is off. Use tongs or pliers to retrieve an object, and always switch off the breaker before working on the unit. A disposal is a motor with a grinding plate, and a switch can be bumped.

If the unit trips its reset button repeatedly, the motor may be failing. Resetting it again and again can overheat the windings, so call us.

Questions we hear from Blue Springs homeowners

Do you repair garbage disposals outside normal hours in Blue Springs?

The line is answered 24/7. If the sink is backing up into the cabinet, shut off the disposal and call so we can schedule the earliest visit available.

Why does my disposal hum but not spin?

The blades are jammed. An allen key in the bottom socket can free it, followed by the reset button.

Is it worth repairing an old disposal?

A jam or reset issue is. A leak from the housing is not, and replacement makes sense.

Can you install a disposal where there was none?

Often yes, if there is a switch or power source and the drain layout allows.

Why does my sink back up when the disposal runs?

The clog is likely in the line beyond the disposal.
