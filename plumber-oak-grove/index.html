Start with the weather: freeze season in Oak Grove

Open land and exposed outbuildings mean more pipes face the wind. A garage sink, an outdoor spigot or a well house line can freeze before the main house does. When a pipe splits in a freeze, the water often releases when it thaws, which is why a call after the temperature rises is typical.
