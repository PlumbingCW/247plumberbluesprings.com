Blue Springs plumbing: what the housing and the water mean

Blue Springs grew from about 6,800 residents in 1970 to about 25,900 in 1980 and about 40,200 in 1990, so a large share of the housing stock is from those decades. Homes of that age can have aging supply lines, original water heaters and drain lines with joints that roots have found. Our repiping and sewer inspection pages explain how we approach older plumbing.

The City of Blue Springs buys treated water from Independence, Kansas City and Tri County Water Authority. The state-filed 2025 report lists carbonate hardness of 123 mg/L for the Independence component, about 7 grains per gallon by our conversion, which is moderately hard. That shows up as scale in water heaters and faucets, covered on water heater repair and water softener. Your tap may differ, so test it.

Landmarks and roads help us route a call. Interstate 70, U.S. Route 40, Missouri Route 7 and Adams Dairy Parkway are the main corridors, and Fleming Park, with Blue Springs Lake and Lake Jacomo, sits to the south. If you tell us the nearest cross street, we know where to send the plumber.

Emergency plumber questions in Blue Springs

Do you offer an emergency plumber in Blue Springs at night and on weekends?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Shut off the main water valve if water is spraying, then call so we can schedule the earliest visit available.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Close the main water valve, turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry, move belongings off the floor and stop using any drain that is backing up.

Do you give a price before starting work?

Yes. You get a clear price before work begins. We do not guarantee a fixed arrival time, and we say honestly how long dispatch is expected to take.

Which towns do you serve besides Blue Springs?

Grain Valley, Oak Grove, Lee's Summit and Independence, covering the 64014, 64015, 64029, 64075, 64063, 64064, 64081, 64086, 64055, 64056 and 64057 ZIP codes.

Who handles water and sewer problems on the city side of my property?

The City of Blue Springs water and sewer line, (816) 622-4444. Problems inside the house and on your own lateral are the owner's repair, and we handle those.

Can I book online?

No. This is a call-only line. Phone us and we take the details directly.
